Introduction to Wildlife

Wildlife refers to all living organisms that exist in natural environments. It includes animals, birds, insects, and plants that play a crucial role in maintaining ecological balance.

A Wildlife Management System is designed to protect and manage wild animals and their natural habitats. It helps in monitoring wildlife populations, conserving endangered species, and maintaining ecological balance.

The system focuses on reducing threats such as illegal hunting, habitat loss, and environmental pollution. Wildlife management plays an important role in preserving biodiversity and ensuring that forests, animals, and humans can coexist peacefully.

By using proper planning, conservation methods, and awareness programs, a Wildlife Management System helps protect nature for present and future generations.
